# Game idea

You are the devil, you live together with the Djinn on Behemoth, where you climb up Gaja to flee from the upstream
eating up everything alive.

- You harvest for mana and while doing so, you speed up the upstream.
- You have to pollute earth to weaken Gaja so that you can dig deeper and find more valuable mana
- You try to lower the virtue of humans to move them towards evil and gain more slaves
- You try to gain influence in the human world to lower virtue and to spend on extras, e.g. Fascism
- Under special circumstances, you can declare Fascism for some time. This will give you a huge boost in slaves and pollution
- You trade mana and slaves as main currencies
